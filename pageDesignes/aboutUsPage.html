Our Journey

From a small kitchen to becoming the heart of every Indian family's snack time, our story is baked with love and seasoned with tradition.

The Beginning

Founded with a simple mission: to bring the authentic taste of homemade namkeen to everyone. We started small, focusing purely on quality and that nostalgic flavor.

Growing the Family

As word spread, so did our reach. We expanded our product line to include farali, sev, and unique localized flavors, becoming a staple in regional households.

Modern Innovation

Today, we blend traditional recipes with state-of-the-art manufacturing to ensure every packet of Vraj Namkeen delivers consistent crunch, hygiene, and unparalleled taste.
